About Ian

Hi! I'm Ian, a gameplay programmer from João Pessoa, Brazil. I'm finishing the Tecnólogo em Jogos Digitais (Game Development Associate Degree) program at PUC Minas — three years built around Unity, with the same axis running through every team project I've worked on: the player and the things that interact with the player.

My journey

Across the program I've worked on three team projects, each one deepening the same axis: the player avatar, the controller, the animation pipeline, weapons, damage, enemy behaviour. The arc moves from co-lead gameplay on a first-semester orbital shooter, to architecting state machines and lock-on targeting on a Souls-style action-RPG, to serving as Lead Programmer on a local co-op shooter the team shipped to itch.io.

Long-term I want to work on FPS combat, movement, and player systems — the kind of programming where character feel, weapons, and moment-to-moment decisions are the product. Right now I'm looking for entry-level or junior gameplay programmer roles where I can keep working on that axis with a team further along than I am.

  • Player controllers (3D and orbital)
  • Character setup end-to-end (rig → Animator → IK)
  • Weapons, damage, hit feedback
  • Hierarchical state machines
  • Lock-on targeting (viewport-space)
  • NavMesh enemy AI

Project Roles

My role on each team project across the program. Reverse chronological — most recent first.

Lead Programmer

Dark Side of Horror · 2025 · Final semester · Team of 4

Led the gameplay programming on the team's final-semester project — a local co-op top-down shooter shipped publicly on itch.io.

  • Built the player controller end-to-end (movement, rotation, animator integration, IK).
  • Built a data-driven weapon framework with five weapon types and two fire modes.
  • Built a generic object pool used for bullets, weapon pickups, and VFX.
  • Rebuilt the wave system with a player-count-aware spawn formula and mid-match respawn flow.
  • Unity 6
  • C#
  • Local co-op
  • Weapons
  • Pooling
  • Wave system

Gameplay programmer (state machines & lock-on)

Feneco · 2024 · Intermediate semesters · Team of 8+

Owned the player and enemy state machine architecture and the lock-on targeting system on a Souls-style 3D action-RPG.

  • Built a hierarchical state machine for player (FreeLook, Targeting, Jumping, Falling, Attacking) and enemy (Idle, Patrol, Chasing, Attacking, Impact, Dead).
  • Built lock-on targeting: viewport-space target selection integrated with Cinemachine TargetGroup.
  • Built NavMesh-driven enemy chase (agent computes path, CharacterController applies motion).
  • Built the inventory and item collection system from scratch.
  • Unity
  • C#
  • State machines
  • Lock-on
  • NavMesh
  • Cinemachine

Co-lead gameplay programmer

Survival-Chaos · 2023 · First semester · Team of 6

Co-led gameplay programming on a Resogun-inspired 3D orbital arena shooter, the first-semester team project.

  • Built the orbital coordinate system as a shared primitive across player, enemies, and projectiles.
  • Built the multi-phase boss with three attack patterns and a toggle-driven laser phase.
  • Built enemy movement, four trigger-based collision handlers, and the damage/health flow.
  • Unity
  • C#
  • Orbital motion
  • Projectiles
  • Boss
